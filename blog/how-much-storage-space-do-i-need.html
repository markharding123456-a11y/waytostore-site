How much storage space do I need?

The most common question we get, and the one people most often get wrong in both directions. Here is how to think about it.

Start with the one question that decides it

Do you need to get at your things while they are in storage, or are you sealing the unit and coming back in six months? If you need access, plan for a walkway down the middle and leave a third of the floor open. If you are packing it once and leaving it, you can fill the unit to the door and go a size smaller. Everything else follows from that answer.

A rough guide by unit size

Unit sizes are given as floor dimensions in feet. Most indoor units are about eight feet high, so the height is the free space people forget to use. Stack boxes, stand mattresses on edge, and put a table upside down on top of a dresser.

UnitRoughly the same asWhat fits
5 x 5A hall closetBoxes, seasonal decorations, a bike, camping gear, a few small pieces of furniture.
5 x 10A walk-in closetThe contents of a bachelor suite: a mattress set, a dresser, a small couch, boxes.
10 x 10Half a single garageA one-bedroom apartment, or a large appliance plus a room of furniture.
10 x 15A large bedroomA two-bedroom apartment or a small house, with appliances.
10 x 20A single garageA three-bedroom house, or a vehicle with room around it.
10 x 30A garage and a halfA large house, or a contractor's tools and materials with room to work.

These are working rules, not promises. A household with a piano, a sectional and a workshop needs more than the table says. Call us with a list and we will tell you honestly which size to take.

Three mistakes we see

Renting too small to save money

A unit that is jammed to the door costs you every time you need one thing from the back. If you will visit more than twice, size up one step.

Renting too big out of caution

Most people overestimate. Measure your biggest three items, count your boxes, and remember the unit is eight feet tall. Half-empty units are the most common thing we see.

Forgetting the awkward items

Ladders, kayaks, a bed frame, a rolled carpet. Long items decide the shape of the unit you need more than the number of boxes does. A 5 x 10 takes a ten-foot ladder; a 10 x 10 does not, unless it goes on a diagonal.

What about the compound?

Outdoor spaces work differently. What matters is the length of the RV, boat and trailer, or container, plus the room the tow vehicle needs to swing it in. Give us the overall length including the hitch and we will place you where you can get in and out without a spotter.
